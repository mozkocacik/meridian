# FaqList

Questions that open to show their answer, max `faq-max` (768px). Built on native details and summary, so keyboard and screen reader support come from the browser.

Rows have a line border top and bottom. The question uses `faq-question` (18px, 600, ink) with a chevron in ink-muted that turns when open. The answer is `body` in ink-muted and may hold TextLinks.

Put it under a SectionHeader ("Frequently asked questions") on the pricing page or above the CtaBlock. Four to eight questions. Answer in one to three short sentences.

**Content:** the questions and answers (an answer can contain links), and the item that is open first.
