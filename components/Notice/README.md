# Notice

Product change notices (PCN) and security advisories. They are part of the product page, not news.

1px `border`, radius `r-3`, a 3px inside line on the left in `info` or `warning`. Icon, title (600), one or two sentences in `text-2`, and actions as text links. Info notices can close; security advisories can not.

**Content:** notice number and title, what changes, what to do, links.
