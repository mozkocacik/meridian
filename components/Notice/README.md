# Notice

Product change notices and security advisories are part of the product, not news. They appear on the product page and in the support center.

- `canvas` panel, 1px `border`, radius `r-3`, a 3px inset stripe in the tone color: `info` for change notices, `warning` for advisories.
- A Lucide icon in the tone color, a bold title with the notice number, one or two sentences in `text-2`, then text links.
- A dismiss button only on notices the user has read elsewhere.

The consumer gives the number, title, body and links. Dates are ISO 8601.
