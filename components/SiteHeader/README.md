# SiteHeader

The global navigation of a company site. 64px bar (`header-h`) on `canvas` with a `border` rule: logo, five main sections, language, search, and one primary "Contact sales" button. Below 1024px the sections fold into a menu button.

The current section has a 2px `accent` underline. The mega menu opens under the bar with `shadow-2` and `t-slow`: three columns of product families (overline group label, item name in 560, one-line description in `text-3`) and one featured product with its drawing and lifecycle badge.

Logo: the company's own mark. The sample "Kestrel" mark is a placeholder.
